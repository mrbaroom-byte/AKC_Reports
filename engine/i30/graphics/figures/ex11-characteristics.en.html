Fixed Maturity

The Fund has a fixed maturity date. Accordingly, the Fund Manager seeks to build a portfolio of income-generating investments, consistent with the Fund’s maturity date, that enables it to exit on the maturity date and distribute the Fund’s Net Asset Value to the Unitholders at that time.

Fixed Number of Units

Unlike open-ended mutual funds, closed-ended investment funds issue a fixed number of Units at the time of the Initial Public Offering. Subsequent to the Initial Public Offering, investors are unable to buy or sell Units directly from or to the Fund; they can only buy or sell them on the Saudi Exchange (Tadawul), where Units are bought and sold between investors in the same way as corporate shares.

Trading on the Secondary Market (Tadawul)

Units are listed and traded on the secondary market (Tadawul), providing the opportunity to buy or sell Units as needed.

Distribution

The Fund Manager seeks to distribute the net income generated from the Fund’s underlying investments. The distribution policy provides for distribution of dividends twice per year, and the frequency of distributions may be increased as the Fund Manager deems appropriate, subject to the approval of the Fund Board.

Fund Management

The Fund Manager is responsible for managing the investments and for ensuring that they achieve the Fund’s investment objectives and are consistent with the fixed maturity date.

Target Investors

Closed-ended funds with fixed maturities are designed for investors seeking income flows, recovering the Net Asset Value and exiting the Fund within a specified period.
