Sukuk
  • Global Sukuk allocation covering sovereign and corporate issuers.
  • Upon deployment of capital, the average credit rating of the Sukuk portfolio will be investment grade or higher, according to the ratings of Standard & Poor’s, Fitch or Moody’s.
Asset Backed Debts
  • Exposure to the asset-backed debt market through rated tranches, ensuring that the average credit rating of the portfolio is investment grade or higher at the time of allocation.
  • This investment provides exposure to floating-rate assets with a credit spread and a maturity consistent with the Fund Term.
Trade Finance Contracts Transactions
  • A diversified portfolio of short-term, asset-backed and credit-insured trade finance transactions in global markets.
  • The Fund Manager targets investment in self-liquidating transactions with better risk characteristics, owing to the visibility of cash flows.
Income Funds
  • Exposure to Income Funds: private credit, real estate debt, leasing and/or any asset class consistent with the Fund’s strategy.
  • The investment limits for Income Funds are separate from those of the other asset classes in which the Fund invests directly.
Leasing Transactions
  • Investments will be made in a diversified portfolio of medium-term Leasing Transactions to finance various types of equipment used in sectors including transportation, public utilities, maritime transport, cleaning, food packaging, healthcare, etc. Transactions are executed through a specialized broker in accordance with the Fund Manager’s guidelines.
Murabaha Transactions
  • A short-term allocation that allows efficient liquidity management, to avoid a drag on performance.
  • Investments in the target asset classes may take the form of Murabaha investments, and such allocations will be classified, as applicable, within the limits of the target asset class.
Structured Income Instruments
  • These are securities whose returns are linked to, or affected by, credit securities that may be issued by governments and/or companies, or by private finance transactions.
  • This investment adds further diversification to the Fund’s investments across various global sectors and markets, with a maturity consistent with the Fund Term.
