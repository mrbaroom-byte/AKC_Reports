The Fund
Suitability:
  • Target return and its consistency with the Fund’s objectives.
  • Investment objective, strategy and underlying assets.
  • Focus on geographic and sector diversification.
  • Risk levels.
  • Fee and expense structure.
Fund Managers
Fund Manager Profile:
  • Licensed by the CMA or comparable supervisory authorities.
  • Track record in asset management
  • Market reputation
  • Total assets under management
  • Diversity of the team’s know-how and experience
Performance
Track Record:
  • Historical returns consistent with the target returns
  • Stable distributions of current cash returns
  • Proven track record of success in the target asset types
  • Default rates that generally do not affect the target returns
